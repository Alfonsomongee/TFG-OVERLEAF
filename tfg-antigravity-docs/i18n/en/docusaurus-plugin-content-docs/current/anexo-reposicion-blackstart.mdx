  <AnnexKeyQuestion>
    How were autonomous start-up (Black Start) and reference imports coordinated to restore supply in the Iberian Peninsula after the total collapse?
  </AnnexKeyQuestion>

  <AnnexThesisBox>
    The restoration process of the Iberian Peninsula on April 28 combined two parallel and coordinated strategies: the autonomous start-up (Black Start) of local hydro and thermal plants to form isolated subsystems (restoration islands), and the injection of reference voltage from the French interconnection to re-energize the northern transmission corridor. Despite initial delays in coupling combined cycle units, the use of the continental reference and pumped-storage load shedding allowed 90% of supply to be recovered in less than 10 hours, demonstrating the effectiveness of coordinated contingency plans.
  </AnnexThesisBox>

  <AnnexBlackoutRelevance>
