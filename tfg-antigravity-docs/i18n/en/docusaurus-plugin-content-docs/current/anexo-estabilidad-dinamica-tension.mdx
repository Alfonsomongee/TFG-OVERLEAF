  <AnnexKeyQuestion>
    Why was the loss of voltage stability — and not the frequency drop — the root cause of the April 28 collapse?
  </AnnexKeyQuestion>

  <AnnexThesisBox>
    Records from PMUs installed at the Carmona substation (400 kV) document that voltage began to diverge minutes before frequency left its normal band. Overvoltages in the southern peninsula exceeded the protection limits of IBR inverters, causing massive disconnections that, in turn, caused the frequency drop. The system's reduced inertia — a consequence of the generation mix documented in Annex I — limited the grid's ability to absorb frequency disturbances once the cascade began. The cause-consequence distinction between voltage and frequency is central to the TFG's regulatory recommendations.
  </AnnexThesisBox>

  <AnnexBlackoutRelevance>
    This annex documents the voltage oscillations and PMU records that demonstrate how voltage instability preceded and caused the frequency drop of April 28.
  </AnnexBlackoutRelevance>
