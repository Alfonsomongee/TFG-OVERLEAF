# Economic Impact of the Blackout

Analytical breakdown of the costs and financial responsibilities derived from the massive disconnection of April 28. It considers the valuation of unserved energy (VoLL), the forced injection of backup generation with combined gas cycles, and the direct costs from damages to system assets.
