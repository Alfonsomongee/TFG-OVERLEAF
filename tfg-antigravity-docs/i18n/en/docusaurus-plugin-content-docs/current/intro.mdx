# Welcome

## Technical Analysis of the Iberian Blackout — April 28, 2025

This interactive documentation site presents a detailed technical analysis of the total collapse of the Iberian electrical system that occurred on April 28, 2025 — the most severe systemic incident documented in the history of the European continental electrical system.

### Structure of the Analysis

The research spans **9 chapters** examining:

- **Chapter 1: Introduction** — The object of study and three conflicting narratives (Government/Redeia, Generator sector/ICAI, ENTSO-E)
- **Chapter 2: Background** — Energy transition, evolution of the generation mix, prior operational state
- **Chapter 3: Incident Anatomy** — Detailed chronology of the 4 collapse phases
- **Chapter 4: Response and Restoration** — Operational response and system recovery
- **Chapter 5: Report Analysis** — Confrontation of diverging technical perspectives
- **Chapter 6: Communication Impact** — Analysis of institutional narratives
- **Chapter 7: Resilience & Future** — Lessons and regulatory recommendations
- **Chapter 8: AI Usage in the Thesis** — Methodology and LLM physical validation protocols
- **Chapter 9: Conclusions** — Synthesis of findings and future implications

### Central Theme

The analysis investigates how a modern electrical system, with over 82% instantaneous <GlossaryLink term="IBR">renewable penetration</GlossaryLink>, collapsed in just **11 seconds** (between 12:33:18 and 12:33:29 CEST) during a cascading disconnection sequence triggered by overvoltage — despite having adequate frequency margins.

**Core debate:** Was the incident the result of **<GlossaryLink term="Colapso Q-V">primarily capacitive voltage instability</GlossaryLink>** (IIT-ICAI, NREL) or of **operational regulatory non-compliance** (Government, REE)?
