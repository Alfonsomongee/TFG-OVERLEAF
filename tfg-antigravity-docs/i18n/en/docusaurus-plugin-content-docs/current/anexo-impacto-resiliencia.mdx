  <AnnexKeyQuestion>
    What was the real socioeconomic cost of the April 28 blackout, and what lessons about sectoral and institutional resilience can be drawn from the collapse?
  </AnnexKeyQuestion>

  <AnnexThesisBox>
    The April 28 blackout caused estimated losses of over 240 million euros, primarily affecting the manufacturing sector and metropolitan transport. Comparison with other historical collapses shows that, although the total restoration time was relatively short (less than 10 hours) thanks to cross-border support, the vulnerability of the industrial fabric highlighted the need to review the Value of Lost Load (VoLL) standards. The regulatory sanction schemes (CNMC) and the energy trilemma balance require a thorough reassessment following the event.
  </AnnexThesisBox>

  <AnnexBlackoutRelevance>
    This annex compiles sectoral losses and a resilience comparison of the Iberian system against large-scale interruption events internationally.
  </AnnexBlackoutRelevance>
