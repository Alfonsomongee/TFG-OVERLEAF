<GlitchTitle>Technical Glossary</GlitchTitle>

:::note
This glossary compiles the main technical terms from the analysis of the 28-A event, defined based on standards, grid codes, and reference technical literature, including IEEE Std 2800-2022, ENTSO-E NC RfG, CIGRE, and Kundur (1994). Each term includes its formal definition, its regulatory or bibliographic reference, its specific relevance to the Iberian collapse, and expanded technical details.

For a broader coverage of terminology —approximately 119 terms—, the floating panel available by hovering over or activating the underlined terms in the text of each chapter provides contextual definitions at reading time.
:::
