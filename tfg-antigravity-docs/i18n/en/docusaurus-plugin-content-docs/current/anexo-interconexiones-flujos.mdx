  <AnnexKeyQuestion>
    What role did cross-border interconnections play in the propagation of instability and the subsequent restoration of the Iberian system?
  </AnnexKeyQuestion>

  <AnnexThesisBox>
    During the 28-A collapse, interconnections played a critical dual role. Initially, when the imbalance occurred due to the loss of Iberian synchronous generation, import flows from France increased sharply, exceeding transfer capacity and causing loss of synchronism on the Pyrenean border. However, in the restoration phase, the interconnection with France acted as the main vector for re-energizing the Spanish transmission grid, allowing key substations to be brought online long before local reserve power plants were available.
  </AnnexThesisBox>

  <AnnexBlackoutRelevance>
    This annex brings together the tie-line power flows and the continental loss-of-synchronism events that determined the electrical isolation of the Iberian Peninsula on 28-A.
  </AnnexBlackoutRelevance>
