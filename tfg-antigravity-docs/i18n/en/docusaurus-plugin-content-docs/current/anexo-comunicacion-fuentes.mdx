  <AnnexKeyQuestion>
    How did information biases and the asymmetry of official communication influence citizens' perception of the electrical system's security after April 28?
  </AnnexKeyQuestion>

  <AnnexThesisBox>
    The public treatment of the April 28 blackout was marked by strong narrative polarization. While conservative media prematurely emphasized the culpability of the renewable model and the ecological transition, progressive media justified the incident as a fortuitous failure unrelated to market design. The delay in the system operator's official reports and the contradictions between REE's press releases and the Government Committee fueled conspiracy theories and temporarily reduced public confidence in the system's robustness.
  </AnnexThesisBox>

  <AnnexBlackoutRelevance>
